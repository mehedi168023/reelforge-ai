# Icon and scene recipes

Worked scenes from the original film load with `read_skill examples/<name>`. They were made for a desktop engine: ignore `data-*` attributes, `FF.t.local` cue times and `window.__timelines`, and use your own times on `window.__tl`. Replace any CSS filter or blur swap with opacity and transform. Read the file for the technique you need. They depend on that film's cue names and carry that film's text, amounts and brand. Copy the moves only. A number in an example, such as the ৳১৩ ticket in `free.html`, came from that client's app and means nothing for yours.

| Example | What it shows | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| `hook.html` | A scope hunts a heart; blur swap on one stage; a wallet with a hinged flap, coins leaving and returning, a tick stamp | Opening hook, "no more X" lines |
| `brand.html` | Logo sting: energy gathers, slam, white frame, ring, sparks, lightning draw-on, masked sheen | Any logo reveal |
| `welcome.html` | A form fills and a cursor taps; a gift trembles and the lid blows off; coin burst; count-up; a spinning coin splits into two tickets | Sign-up offers, bonuses, one thing becoming two |
| `referral.html` | A network grows one friend at a time; coins ride back along each line; a range lands number by number | Referral, sharing, network effects |
| `free.html` | Calendar days light in sequence; stamp slam; a price struck out beside a torn stub; a game pad played while a skill bar fills; a trophy rises into rays with confetti | "Every day", "free", "show your skill", prizes |

| `pc.html` | A product builds itself part by part, powers on, fans spin up, spotlights sweep; a speech bubble answers disbelief; a rule gets a tick | Prize or hardware reveals |

## Picking a shape for a line

| The line says | Try |
|---|---|
| An amount of money | Count-up or slam, with coins and rays; keep any "about" or "up to" on screen |
| A list named one by one | One diagram that grows, each part waking on its name |
| Something is removed or free | Strike-out or stamp, then the new value |
| A warning or a pause | Hazard tape, red tint, three-frame shake |
| A system working | A machine that visibly runs: gears, filling bars, typing digits |
| A promise of safety | Threat, detection, then a shield and a colour change |
| "Download" or "visit" | A device acting it out, then the address held on screen |
