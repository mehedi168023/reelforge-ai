# Clean Tips

Modelled on a study of a short-form "filming tips" video. It gets its energy from cuts and clarity, not effects. Use the style only, never another creator's footage, name or assets.

## The look

- **Captions:** one to three words at a time, centred at mid-frame or just below the face, heavy clean sans-serif (Inter or Montserrat 800-900), white with a soft dark shadow. Lowercase or sentence case, not all caps. The word changes on the beat of speech, with no bounce.
- **Chapter cards:** a full-frame flat white card with centred heavy black caps, such as `NUMBER 3 / BLUR YOUR BACKGROUND`. Hold 0.8-1.2 s, enough to read. Hard cut in, hard cut out. One per tip.
- **Cutaways:** when the speaker names an object or action, cut to it for 1.5-3 s. Use the person's B-roll if supplied. Without it, build a clean flat graphic of that object and say that you drew it.
- **Product and text cards:** a centred square card on black with a thin outline, a heavy title and a small subtitle, held while the speech keeps going.
- **Colour:** white, black and the footage's own colours. One accent at most.

## Motion

Hard cuts carry it. Captions appear with no slide, or a 2-frame scale from 1.04 to 1. Punch in 1.0 -> 1.1 on a new point, as a hard cut. Keep the frame busy by changing something every 1-2 s, but never move a word that is being read.

## Sound

Soft pop on chapter cards, light whoosh on cutaways, and nothing on captions. Keep it restrained, because the voice carries this style.

## Mistakes to avoid

Don't add glow, gradients or animated backgrounds to cards. A card that needs a second read is too long, so cut its words.
